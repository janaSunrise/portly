## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-21

First version.

### Added

- Menu bar panel listing everything listening on a TCP port, read straight from the kernel
- Projects, Apps and Services sections. System processes are hidden, and Services can be collapsed
- Search by port, name or folder
- Right-click to open in browser, copy URL, command or PID, reveal in Finder, kill or force kill
- ⌘⌫ to kill the selected row, double-click or Return to open it
- A marker on ports that are reachable from your network
- Launch at login
- About window
